## 1. Site audit (before changes)

- **Positioning.** The site was framed almost entirely around training and capability. Business analysis, systems and operating-model work were invisible. The footer said "Learning Designed for Impact", not "Setting the conditions for success".
- **Navigation.** It was crowded: a Sectors dropdown, seven links and a "Capability Review" CTA. Buyers met sectors and methods before they met what Prelude does.
- **Tone.** "™" appeared on every framework, alongside "Our intellectual property", a "vs a large consultancy" comparison and a sticky CTA bar with a close button that behaved like a pop-up. All of this was more sales-heavy than an evidence-led consultancy needs.
- **Claims.** The homepage "Evidence, not promises" band showed figures that aren't documented anywhere in the brief or book: up to 25% performance, +20% readiness and 18% fewer compliance gaps.
- **Typography.** H1 reached 5.4rem. Eyebrows were tracked at .34em, which made them hard to read. Sections used `<p class="lead">` as visual headings, so the heading outline was weak.
- **Spacing.** Sections used a fixed 120px rhythm, and content was largely card-based.
- **Accessibility.** The mobile menu was a `div role="button"` with no `aria-expanded` state and no Escape key handling. Dropdown buttons and the burger were under 44px tall. There were no explicit focus styles.
- **Book.** There was no book page and no toolkit, even though the printed book sends readers to `prelude-learning.com/book-toolkit`.
- **Analytics.** None were installed. The privacy policy says there are no analytics or tracking cookies.

## 3. Typography and spacing

- **Body text:** `clamp(1rem, .94rem + .25vw, 1.125rem)`, which runs from 16px on mobile to 18px on desktop. Line height is 1.65 and colour is `#D6D4CB` on forest green (about 12:1 contrast). Paragraphs are capped at 72ch.
- **Headings:**
  - H1: `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.75rem)`. Page heroes use `clamp(2.25rem, 4.4vw, 4rem)`.
  - H2: `clamp(2rem, 3.5vw, 3.25rem)`.
  - H3: `clamp(1.25rem, 2vw, 1.65rem)`.
  - Eyebrows: 13px with .16em tracking.
- **Section rhythm:** `--sec-y` is 104px on desktop, 80px at 1024px and below, and 56px at 600px and below.
- **Widths:** content max width is 1240px, and long prose is limited to 720px.
- **Components:** new sections use rules and lists rather than cards. Cards are kept only for genuinely separate items, such as the downloads panel.
- **Interaction:** visible gold focus rings throughout, and touch targets of at least 44px.

## 5. Toolkit implementation

- **Hosting.** It runs on GoDaddy cPanel (Apache and PHP 7.4 or newer). No database or third-party form service is needed.
- **Submissions.** `submit.php` validates the form on the server and checks a honeypot field. It also applies a rate limit of 8 requests per hour per hashed IP address.
- **Data storage.** Requests are written to `prelude-private/data/toolkit-requests.csv`, outside `public_html`, with file locking and protection against spreadsheet formula injection. Opt-in marketing consent is recorded **separately** in `marketing-consent.csv`, with the exact wording and its version. Consent is never required for access.
- **Redirect.** On success the handler sets a strictly necessary access cookie and sends a `303` redirect straight to `/book-toolkit/downloads/`. The cookie holds a random ID, an expiry date and a signature, valid for 12 months. The email is sent afterwards, so the visitor never waits for it.
- **Email.** It is sent through Resend if an API key is configured, and otherwise through the host's `mail()`. It goes out from `jason.smith@prelude-learning.com` using the brief's wording. Failed sends are logged to `email-failures.csv`.
- **Email link.** The link is `/downloads/?t=<token>`. The page sets the cookie and then redirects to the clean URL, so the token doesn't stay in the address bar. An invalid or expired token goes back to the form with an explanation.
- **Files.** Files are kept in `prelude-private/toolkit/` and served by `download.php`, which checks access and serves only IDs listed in the manifest. It sends the correct file type, a sensible download filename and `nosniff`, and server paths are never exposed. If a file hasn't been uploaded yet, the visitor sees a friendly message.
- **Manifest.** `book-toolkit/lib/manifest.json` is generated by build.py, so the page labels and the handler can't drift apart. The `lib` folder is blocked from public access.
- **Settings.** The only required setting is a `secret` of at least 32 random characters. `mail_from` and `resend_api_key` are optional. These can be set either as environment variables or in `prelude-private/config.php`. The template is in `book-toolkit/lib/config.example.php`.
- **Setup steps** are in `README-TOOLKIT.md`.
