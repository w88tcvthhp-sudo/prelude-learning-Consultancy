**Purpose:** a record of every factual or commercial claim on the website that a prospective client, partner or auditor could reasonably ask Prelude to support. For each claim it shows what evidence Prelude holds, how the claim is now presented, and what still needs to happen.

**Owner:** Jason Smith. **Last reviewed:** 9 October 2026 (Phase 1).

**Rule applied:** no claim is published as a Prelude result unless Prelude can support it. Figures from the founder's previous roles are shown only where the reader can see they were reported by the organisation at the time.

## 1. Outcome figures from case studies

All eight case studies are **founder-led experience**: work led or delivered by Jason Smith in previous roles or as part of wider teams. None was a Prelude Ltd contract (decision 5). The figures were measured by the organisations involved; Prelude does not hold the underlying data (decision 1).

**Where the figures now appear:** only on the individual case-study page and on the case-study hub, each beside this note: *"Figures were measured and reported by the organisation at the time of the work. Prelude does not hold the underlying data, and the baseline, method and period are not published here."* They have been removed from service, sector, pillar, article and About pages and from the homepage.
