Four levels of analysis

Use the smallest level that gives you a defensible decision.

These are different levels of analytical depth, not a sequence. A request does not need to progress through all four.

  1. 60 minutes

    Triage the request.

  2. One day

    Reach a defensible initial diagnosis.

  3. Five days

    Conduct evidence-based analysis of a significant requirement.

  4. Full TNA

    Complete comprehensive analysis for complex or high-risk requirements.
