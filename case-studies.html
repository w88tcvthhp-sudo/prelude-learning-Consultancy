Anonymised Prelude exampleBusiness Analysis & Improvement

Owner-led service business

Initial request
Improve the digital presence and generate more enquiries.
What the analysis revealed
The more important question was what would happen if demand actually increased. The binding constraints were owner dependency, pricing, systems, operating capacity, delegation and quality control.
Work expanded into
Business and growth strategy · operating model · systems requirements and options analysis · processes and policies · workforce structure and quality · implementation planning

Anonymised Prelude exampleBusiness Analysis & Improvement

Digital platform / start-up

Initial request
An independent product and UX review.
What the analysis revealed
The product had progressed further than some of the proposition and behavioural assumptions underneath it.
Work examined
Proposition · users · business model · user journeys and UX/UI · trust · accessibility risks · requirements · evidence and assumptions · measures · priorities · launch readiness
Outcome of the review
A narrower, evidence-led route forward: prove the core proposition before investing in further breadth. The recommendation was not simply “build more”.
