Founder-led experienceDefence · capability and TNA

MOD Digital Skills for Defence (DS4D)

Challenge. Defence was framing a digital skills problem as a training problem, when the real question was what digital capability it actually required.

Contribution. Jason led the DSAT-aligned Training Needs Analysis, then helped define the capability requirement, a skills and behaviours framework and a learning architecture aligned to strategic outcomes.

Anonymised Prelude exampleOwner-led business · business analysis

Owner-led service business

Challenge. The request was to improve the digital presence and generate more enquiries. The more important question was what would happen if demand actually increased.

Contribution. Analysis showed the binding constraints were owner dependency, pricing, systems, capacity, delegation and quality control, so the work expanded into growth strategy, the operating model, systems and implementation planning.
