BEFORE YOU STARTAbout this workbook

Most organisations know something isn’t working. Fewer can say with confidence what the problem is, what is causing it and what success would look like. This workbook tests how clearly you can answer those questions before you commit money and time to a solution.

Purpose

The Capability Readiness Review™ is the first step Prelude takes before any investment in training, consultancy, systems or organisational change. This workbook contains the same ten questions, response options and scoring method as the free online self-assessment at prelude-learning.com, so the two give the same result.

Who should complete it

The sponsor or manager who owns the problem, ideally with two or three others who see it from different angles: someone who does the work, someone who manages it and someone who will judge whether it has been fixed. Differences in your answers are often the most useful finding.

How to use it

  1. Agree, in one sentence, the issue you are assessing. Write it in the box below.
  2. Answer each of the ten questions honestly. Tick one response per question. “Not sure” is a legitimate answer and scores zero.
  3. Use the evidence prompts to note what supports your answer. If you can’t point to evidence, consider whether “Partly” or “Not really” is more accurate.
  4. Calculate your score on the scoring worksheet and read the interpretation.
  5. Complete the risk and action-planning pages for any questions you scored at 0 or 1.

What this self-assessment can and cannot tell you

This is an indicative self-assessment. The result is based only on your own answers. It is not an independently validated audit, it has not been statistically validated as a measurement instrument, and it does not establish compliance with any standard. Its value lies in the conversation it prompts and the gaps it exposes.

PART 3Interpreting your result

The bands below are the same as those used by the online self-assessment.

80–100%Strong readinessYou have real clarity on the problem and how to solve it. The priority now is execution and assurance: making sure the right capability is built and measured.
60–79%Developing readinessThe foundations are there, but a few gaps could undermine your investment. Closing them before you commit will sharpen the outcome.
40–59%At riskThere’s enough uncertainty here that investing in a solution now carries real risk. A structured review would protect the spend and the outcome.
0–39%High risk: not yet readyThe real problem isn’t yet defined clearly enough to solve. This is where a facilitated Capability Readiness Review is most useful.

A high score is only as reliable as the evidence behind it. If several of your “Yes, clearly” answers rest on assumption, treat the result as one band lower.
