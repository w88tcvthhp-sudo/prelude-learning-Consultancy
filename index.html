Founder-led experienceDefence

MOD Digital Skills for Defence (DS4D)

Challenge. Defence was framing a digital skills problem as a training problem, when the real question was what digital capability it actually required.

Contribution. Jason led the DSAT-aligned Training Needs Analysis, then helped define the capability requirement, a skills and behaviours framework and a learning architecture aligned to strategic outcomes.

Healthcare Learning Transformation

Challenge. Learning compliance data across around 15,000 colleagues could not be trusted, so leaders were managing risk without reliable visibility.

Contribution. Redesigned Totara dashboards, role-based learning pathways and information management, so leaders could rely on their own compliance reporting.
