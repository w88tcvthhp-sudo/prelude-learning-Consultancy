The problem

An LMS that frustrates more than it helps is a common complaint, but the deeper issue is usually that compliance reporting can't be trusted and learning data has poor visibility — which means leaders are managing risk blind, even though the data technically exists.

How I diagnose it

I ask a direct question: do you trust the numbers your LMS produces enough to put them in a board report unchecked? Very often the honest answer is no — and the cause is usually configuration and information management, not the platform itself.

My approach

LMS optimisation — dashboards, pathways and information management, including Totara — that turns your existing platform into reliable capability intelligence rather than recommending a costly re-platform as the default fix.
