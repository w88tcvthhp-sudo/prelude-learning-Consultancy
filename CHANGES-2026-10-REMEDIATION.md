# Prelude website: remediation, resources and positioning (October 2026)

This round builds on the `2026-10 refinement` commit (`b2342ae`). All pages are still generated by `build.py`. The new code sits in the block marked `2026-10 REMEDIATION`, plus a small number of in-place edits that are listed below. Run `python3 build.py` after editing.

## 1. Decisions taken with Jason (8 October 2026)

- **Quantified case-study claims.** Keep the figures, but label them. The animated count-up headline figures have been removed. Each figure is now shown as a *reported outcome*, with a note that the baseline, measurement method and period are not published.
- **Case-study basis.** The basis is mixed. All eight studies are labelled *Founder-led experience* (work led by Jason, including in previous roles and as part of wider teams). The two anonymised examples are labelled *Anonymised Prelude example*. To relabel a study as a Prelude engagement, change `basis` in `CASE_EXTRAS` in `build.py`.
- **Email gating.** The Capability Readiness Review workbook is a direct download. The other four resources need an email address.
- **Hosting.** The site deploys from GitHub to Vercel. The resource gate therefore uses the existing Formspree form and needs no server code. The toolkit's PHP does not run on Vercel: see section 6.

## 5. Terminology: recommended definitions

| Term | What it is | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Prelude Capability Model™ | A **model** of what capability is made of: six layers from Mission & Outcomes to Performance Evidence. | Keep as the conceptual model. Don't attach a score to it until one is designed and tested. |
| Prelude Performance & Capability Cycle | The **method**: Understand, Diagnose, Define, Intervene, Prove. Used in the book and on Approach. | Make this the single named method. |
| Capability Improvement Approach™ | An older six-step method (Mission, Gap, Root Cause, Intervention, Impact, Improve), still on How I Work and the methodology strip. | Overlaps with the Cycle. Retire it, or show it explicitly as the Cycle's steps, to avoid two methods. |
| Capability Readiness Review™ | The **entry diagnostic tool**: ten questions, at three levels (self-assessment, facilitated review, full diagnostic). The site also shows a "six dimensions" wheel. | Keep. Describe the six areas as *where the problem may sit* (as the playbook now does), not as a scored dimension set. |
| Capability Diagnostic™ / Capability Diagnostic Framework™ | The third CRR level (an engagement), and a separate five-tier graphic. Tool 07 in the book is also called "Capability Diagnostic". | Three uses of one name. Rename the graphic, or retire it in favour of the Capability Model. |

No methodology has been renamed in this round.
